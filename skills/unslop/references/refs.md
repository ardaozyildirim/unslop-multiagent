# References

Finished screens by Damir Sokolovsky ([@heydamir](https://x.com/heydamir)). Look at them before the Visuals pass. Take their qualities, never their subject, palette or layout, so every product still gets its own screen. `unslop-dashboard-after` is the finished version of `examples/dashboard.html`, so when you unslop that example, find your own answer instead of rebuilding it.

| File | What it shows | Why it works |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [unslop-hero-after.webp](refs/unslop-hero-after.webp) | An invoicing hero with a paper plane drawn in digits and currency signs, small notes pinned to it, and the product rising from below | The visual is made of the product's own material, money, so it is the moment and the signature at once. Text sits on calm sky, the chrome is quiet, and the product view invites a scroll |
| [unslop-dashboard-after.webp](refs/unslop-dashboard-after.webp) | A finance dashboard with two rendered payment cards and a revenue chart drawn as soft colored curves over a mirrored grey comparison | Quiet graphite chrome, and all the color lives in two moments, a real looking object and a chart where every color means a series |
| [beacon-engraving.webp](refs/beacon-engraving.webp) | A lighthouse as a blue engraving on a pale tinted page | A print style working because the whole page is in it. One ink, one paper tone, lots of air |
| [lumen-light.webp](refs/lumen-light.webp) | A beam of white light splitting through a glass prism on black | One physical phenomenon as the entire visual, with technical captions in small mono at the edges |
| [margin-object.webp](refs/margin-object.webp) | A notebook app shown as a real hardcover notebook on a desk with leaf shadows, a cup and a pencil | The product becomes an object with weight and light. The scene is calm and the headline sits on the empty part of the desk |
| [doorway-cinematic.webp](refs/doorway-cinematic.webp) | A single lit doorway standing in a dark field | One subject, one light source, one feeling. The headline needs nothing else around it |
| [meadow-3d.webp](refs/meadow-3d.webp) | A soft 3D meadow with a few matte crystal shapes and a huge wordmark sinking into the grass | Friendly stylized 3D with a restrained palette, and type that becomes part of the scene |
| [typewriter-object.webp](refs/typewriter-object.webp) | A typing speed app shown as a real typewriter on a light page with a slab serif headline | The product's world decides the type, the object and the palette, on a light theme |
| [soft-static-vinyl.webp](refs/soft-static-vinyl.webp) | A music app with a bright vinyl record and lyrics written as note names in mono | One very saturated object on black carries all the color, and the signature detail is typographic |
| [story-dashboard.mp4](refs/story-dashboard.mp4) and [story-dashboard-frames.webp](refs/story-dashboard-frames.webp) | The viral story video, a vibecoded dashboard unslopping itself in about 10 seconds, and nine frames from it | Everything morphs in place in a wave, red redlines measure the slop and turn white when fixed, copy rolls letter by letter, and the frame is square and cropped close on the sidebar and first cards |

What they share: one bright element per screen, quiet chrome around it, a visual that can be explained in one sentence, and headlines placed on space that was left empty for them.
